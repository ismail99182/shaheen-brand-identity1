03 / BRAND USAGE GUIDELINES

Logo Usage Rules & Standard Matrix (Dos & Don'ts)

Maintain brand integrity. Never alter geometric proportions, gradient colors, or clear space safety zones.

DO: Clear Safe Zone
Minimum Clear Space X

Maintain clear space equal to the height of the top wing apex around all sides of the mark.

DON'T: Distort Aspect Ratio

Never stretch, squash, or modify the proportions of the wing geometry.

DON'T: Low Contrast Dark

Never place low-contrast dark variants over dark surfaces without proper luminescence.

DON'T: Rotate or Angle

Never rotate, tilt, or apply unapproved drop shadows to the brand emblem.

04 / CONCEPT EXPLORATION

3 Design Directions Evaluated

Each concept explores a different geometric balance between initials SH, falcon wing aesthetics, and modern tech minimalism.

SELECTED WINNER

Concept 1: Monolithic Wing

S + H + 45° Falcon Blade

Rationale: 3 chamfered geometric facets intersect to construct an implicit 'S' loop on the left and 'H' stem on the right. The top right blade extends upward at 45°, creating an iconic falcon wing apex. Ultra-clean and readable at 16px.

Score: 9.9/10

Concept 2: Kinetic Wing Loop

Continuous S-Ribbon + Sharp Apex

Rationale: Emphasizes smooth continuous data flow and cloud computing. The curve forms an 'S' while the triangle corner introduces the hawk wing. Extremely fluid, but slightly less corporate than Concept 1.

Score: 9.2/10

Concept 3: Dual Pillar Falcon

Bold H-Frame + Cut Wing

Rationale: Focuses on enterprise stability and structural code architecture. Two primary columns form 'H', with an overlapping diagonal wing cut. Highly robust and solid.

Score: 8.9/10
